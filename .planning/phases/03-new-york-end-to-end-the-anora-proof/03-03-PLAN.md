<objective>
Close SHP-14's second half: prove the validation suite is non-vacuous, automatically, on every
commit.

Purpose: SHP-14's first half — "a validation test suite runs in CI on every commit, asserting
exact equality against disclosed government figures" — is already satisfied by Phase 1's `tests`
job. The second half is the one that matters and the one nobody usually builds: *and is proven
non-vacuous by deliberately breaking a rule value and confirming the suite catches it*. A
one-time documented ritual proves the suite was non-vacuous on the day someone ran it; suites go
vacuous later, when a fixture flips to blocked, a test is skipped, or a parametrize list quietly
empties. This plan makes that proof a CI log rather than a re-enactment, which is also what
Phase 8's re-proof of SHP-14 reruns.

The mutation is one basis point on New York's credit rate. That size is the point: large enough
that exact `Decimal` equality catches it, small enough that a tolerance-based or eyeball check
would not. It tests the specific claim SHP-14 makes.

Output: a declared mutation table, a five-step bash gate matching the existing CI script family,
and a sixth blocking job in the existing workflow.
</objective>
