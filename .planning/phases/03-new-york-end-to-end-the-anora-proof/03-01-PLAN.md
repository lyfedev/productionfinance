<objective>
Land the phase's tracer: the first real, cited government figure on the hosted URL. Route B —
"Reproduce a disclosure" — reads a committed validation-pair fixture, prices it through the
already-proven Phase 2 engine, and shows the disclosed figure, the computed figure and an
explicit match verdict side by side with both provenance chains.

Purpose: this is success criterion 3 and the demo's opening beat. The engine work is already
done — `price_jurisdiction(load_ruleset("jurisdictions/us-ny.yaml"), Decimal("3964760"))`
returns `Decimal('991190')` today. This plan exposes it end-to-end through every layer the
phase touches (dependency manifest, engine serializer, service, router, template, app wiring,
test) so the architecture is proven on one real path before Route A expands out from it.

The load-bearing distinction this route rests on is **D-31**: a disclosed qualified spend is a
government-published historical fact carrying a URL, a report period and a sha256 — a citation,
not an input. INP-08 governs forward-looking budget figures a producer supplies and has nothing
to say about a disclosure. That is why Route B may feed `$3,964,760` into the engine while the
budget field on Route A is refused, and why the two routes are visibly distinct rather than one
blended form (**D-32**).

Output: two new runtime dependencies, a recursive `Figure` serializer, an `app/services` +
`app/routers` layer, four Jinja2 templates, and a test file that asserts `Decimal("991190")`
through the HTTP boundary.
</objective>
