# Plan 08-01 — the proof panel, the accuracy figure, and unresolved conflicts

## Objective

Make the product's central claim inspectable in-product: a government figure
reproduced exactly, shown next to the government document it came from.

## Why the document itself, not a link

`sources/` already holds byte-archived government documents with re-derivable
sha256 hashes, manifested in `sources/MANIFEST.yaml`. Serve those.

Connecticut's DECD page already 404s — the portal was restructured — which is
precisely why a citation is not proof. The archived artifact plus its hash is
what survives. A live link may sit alongside it as a courtesy.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — the proof panel (UI-07, DMO-01)

`app/routers/proof.py` + `app/templates/proof.html`. For a validation pair:
the disclosed government figure, the engine's reproduced figure, their exact
Decimal difference, and the archived source document with its sha256.

Show the residue exactly. New Jersey's *Joker* pair has a residue of exactly
`Decimal("122665")` — the sourced 2% diversity bonus — and that is a more
convincing artifact than a rounded "matches!" because it demonstrates the model
knows why it differs.

`<verify>`: for a pair that reproduces exactly, the difference renders as zero;
for one with a known residue, the exact residue renders with its explanation.

### Task 2 — the accuracy figure in-product (PRV-06)

Render `agent/taxonomy.py::AccuracySummary` on the hosted page as three counts:
exact match, explained variance, unexplained.

**No blended percentage.** `AccuracySummary` has no percentage field and a test
enforces its absence — do not compute one in the template as a convenience. A
single "94% accurate" headline folds unexplained mismatches into an average and
hides exactly what a reviewer needs to see.

`<verify>`: assert the rendered page contains the three counts and that no
percent sign appears adjacent to an accuracy figure.

### Task 3 — unresolved source conflicts (PRV-07)

`app/templates/_conflict.html`. Where two authoritative sources disagree on a
value, show both with their citations and state plainly that the conflict is
unresolved.

**Do not resolve it.** No winner, no average, no silent preference for the more
recent or the higher-confidence source. This is the same discipline as
`engine.net_cash.transferable` refusing to convert without a sourced discount
rate: the honest output of a disagreement is the disagreement.

Equally: **do not manufacture a conflict** to demonstrate the feature. It
renders only for real recorded disagreements. If none exists in the committed
data, the surface stays empty and a test asserts it renders correctly when
given a real conflict fixture.

## Prohibitions

Never fabricate a figure, a source, a hash, or a conflict.
No React, no Vite, no build step.
`.github/scripts/vendor-scan.sh` cannot tell a comment from a call site.
