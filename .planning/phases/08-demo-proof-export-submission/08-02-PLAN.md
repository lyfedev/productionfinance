# Plan 08-02 — the export document and the four demo beats

## Objective

Deliver UI-09 (export) and stage the four demo beats (DMO-01..04) on the hosted
URL so they are showable rather than described.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — the export document (UI-09)

`app/services/export.py` + `app/routers/export.py`. A comparison becomes a
self-contained document: the two-band ranked list, the decomposed gap, every
figure with its source and date, the assumptions panel, and the D-60
acknowledged-gaps list naming what the model does not price.

Generate it server-side. A producer forwards this to someone who will open it
without running our JavaScript.

The gaps list travelling with the document is not optional garnish — a document
that omits what it does not price is the document this product exists to
replace.

### Task 2 — DMO-02 and DMO-03, the two arithmetic beats

**DMO-02:** the naive-percentage case. Show the naive figure, the correct
figure, and name why they diverge. The brief cites a UK example overstating by
44%. Use real committed data; if the exact £18M example is not in the repo,
use a real case that is and state its actual overstatement rather than quoting
a number the data does not support.

**DMO-03:** the ranking inversion. Show both orderings — by headline rate, and
by net cash with timing — so a viewer sees the inversion happen rather than
being told it does. This is the product's whole thesis in one screen.

### Task 3 — DMO-04, the live-research beat

Wire the existing `/research` surface into the demo page as the fourth beat: an
uncurated city researched live, priced, labelled unvalidated and visibly
distinct from government-validated jurisdictions.

**If `PARALLEL_API_KEY` / `GEMINI_API_KEY` are absent, this beat renders an
explicit unavailable state naming the missing credential.** Do not fake it, do
not pre-record a run and present it as live, do not stub the SDK and dress the
output as real. The project brief names a `sleep()` behind a progress bar as a
Stage One death, and this is the same failure with more steps.

A visible "requires credentials, not yet configured" is an honest demo. A
simulated one discovered by a judge ends the submission.

## Prohibitions

Never fabricate a figure, a research run, or a live call.
No React, no Vite, no build step.
`.github/scripts/vendor-scan.sh` cannot tell a comment from a call site.
