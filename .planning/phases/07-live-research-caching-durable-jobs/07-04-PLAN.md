<objective>
Make AGT-10's caching boundary real rather than declared. Plan 07-01 landed the policy
table and used it to assert that live research reads no cache; this plan gives the other
three live data classes genuine request-time resolvers and proves, structurally, that no
module outside `app/services/cache_policy.py` decides cached-versus-live.

Curated rule models stay cached — they are committed YAML whose git history is the audit
trail, which is the whole reason they live in the repo. Cap consumption, programme
open/closed status and FX all move between one request and the next, so each is resolved on
the request, and each says plainly when it could not be.

Purpose: AGT-10 (one enforcement point, four live classes, one cached class), D-89 (the
resolvers execute on a live user request), D-94 (an undetermined answer is reported, never
guessed).

Output: `app/services/live_fx.py`, `agent/live_checks.py`, the completed policy module,
the uncurated-city entry path into `/research`, and the single-point gate test.
</objective>
