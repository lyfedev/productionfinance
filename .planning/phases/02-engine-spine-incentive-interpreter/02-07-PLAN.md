<assumption_delta_decision>
**The signal:** the deterministic detector fired on a pluralization of "alongside", from
ROADMAP SC1's prose "with an estimated cash arrival date reported alongside the value".

**Decision on that signal: `no-change`.** It is a lexical false positive — "alongside"
there is ordinary English about reporting one field next to another, not a singular-to-plural
identity transition. No noun changes primacy because of it.

**A real identity-model shift, found on inspection and worth recording:** CR-01 *is* an
identity failure. Two Figures both answer to the name "the base" inside
`compute_gross_credit` — `core_expenditure_figure`, the immutable `Core expenditure
(pre-cap)` Figure captured once at `compute_qualifying_base` construction time, and
`figure`, the running Figure that every adjustment step evolves. Four of the five
adjustment steps read the running one; the `blended_by_ceiling_split` rate branch reads
the frozen one. Two competing sources of truth for one noun, with no name distinguishing
them, is exactly how an adjustment gets discarded while its derivation line survives.

**Primary noun after this plan: the running, adjusted base.** `figure.value` is the
authority for what has actually been reduced. `core_expenditure_figure` is demoted to
what it was always meant to be — one immutable *input*, consulted solely to re-derive the
percentage cap that SCOPE-FREEZE.md dimension 3 records, never as a stand-in for the base.
The fix below makes that demotion structural rather than a convention a future reader has
to notice: the branch computes an *effective core expenditure* that carries every
non-percentage-cap reduction forward, so there is no longer a pre-adjustment value
reachable from the rate step that could be sliced by accident.
</assumption_delta_decision>
