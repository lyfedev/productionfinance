Output: Twelve fixtures spanning New York, California, New Jersey, Connecticut, Massachusetts and Pennsylvania; three more archived source documents with manifest entries; and a test suite that asserts jurisdiction coverage, the blocked-fixture contract, and per-stage denominators.
